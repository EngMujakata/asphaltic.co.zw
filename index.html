Our Services

Over 16 years of hands-on experience in road surfacing, rehabilitation, and specialized civil works.

Road Surfacing

Specialists in asphaltic road surfacing, concrete paving, and precast pavers. We deliver quality work on time and within specifications.

Pothole Fixing

Dedicated teams equipped to address the pothole challenge across the nation with durable and long-lasting asphalt solutions.

Sport Courts

Construction, maintenance, and repair of tennis courts, volleyball courts, and other tar or concrete surfaced courts.

Road Marking & Signs

Experts in road markings for highways, shopping complexes, parking lots, and industrial safety. We also manufacture and mount all types of road signs.

Durawall Construction

Specialized construction and maintenance of Durawall and precast concrete boundary structures for commercial and residential security.

Asphaltic Plant Hire

Extensive fleet of heavy machinery available for hire, including graders, rollers, excavators, and dump trucks to support any construction project.
